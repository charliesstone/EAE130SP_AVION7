list of changes to openvsp model from original:

- 3/5/26 changed main wing sweep from 35 to 40 to remove interference between main wing and lerx/strake shock during transsonic

- 3/5/26 thickened nose of fuselage to allow for radar

- 3/5/26 removed and redid engine intakes from scratch, now there are two on each side instead of one below. this makes room for internal fuel tank on top of the aircraft as well as hardpoints below, where previously only the internal fuel tank was possible
    - 3/6/26 ducts changed from D-shape ramlip to DSI inline with modern technology standards. typically these are implmeneted in a stealth context, which isnt the focus of our rfp, but they are cheaper than ramlip inlet in addition: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diverterless_supersonic_inlet

- 3/5/26 updated F135 engine to more correctly reflect the geometry and added some detail (fans, diffusers) and variable nozzle

- 3/5/26 added radar, radio avionics internal to fuse
 - 3/6/26 added hydraulic pumps, control computers, and FADEC to fuse

- 3/5/26 added autocannon. nose gear should go somewhere next to autocannon, so barrels were moved starboard to allow for navy nose gear retraction clearance
    - 3/6/26 barrels were moved back to centerline of y for weight and balance purposes. gear has not been added yet, so this might have to change when gear is introduced

- 3/6/26 aircraft was too long by 4 feet, so the ducts and fuselage were shortened around the midline to make total aircraft length 48 ft out of a maximum 50 ft. this is relatively close to maximum, so care should be taken to not exceed jet blast deflection rating

- 3/6/26 total aircraft height is currently at 14 ft out of 18.5. care should be taken to not exceed this limit when introducing gear to the aircraft
