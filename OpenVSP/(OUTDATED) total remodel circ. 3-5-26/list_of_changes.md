list of changes to openvsp model from original:

- changed main wing sweep from 35 to 40 to remove interference between main wing and lerx/strake shock during transsonic

- thickened nose of fuselage to allow for radar

- removed and redid engine intakes from scratch, now there are two on each side instead of one below. this makes room for internal fuel tank on top of the aircraft as well as hardpoints below, where previously only the internal fuel tank was possible

- updated F135 engine to more correctly reflect the geometry and added some detail (fans, diffusers) and variable nozzle

-added radar, radio avionics internal to fuse

-added autocannon. nose gear should go somewhere next to autocannon, so barrels were moved starboard to allow for navy nose gear retraction clearance
